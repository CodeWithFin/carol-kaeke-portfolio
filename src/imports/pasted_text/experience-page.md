Design Principles

Section header: "Design Principles" (serif) with supporting line "These are the principles that guide every product I design." (muted sans).

Layout: 2x2 grid of principle cards (stacking to single column on mobile). Each card should feel like a quiet manifesto statement — generous padding, principle title in medium-large serif, one-sentence explanation below in sans-serif. Consider a subtle number or icon (01–04, or a simple line icon) per card, and a soft hover state (background tint shift to warm sage-tinted paper).


Clarity over complexity — Good design removes uncertainty and helps users move forward with confidence.
Design for trust — Especially in financial products, trust is built through transparency, consistency, and clear communication.
Business and user goals should align — The best products create value for users while supporting measurable business outcomes.
Every interaction should reduce friction — Small improvements across the user journey create meaningful product experiences.
