PROMPT

Design a modern, premium, interactive landing page for a Product Designer's portfolio. The tone should feel confident, editorial, and human — not templated or generic "designer portfolio" boilerplate. Think of it as sitting somewhere between a fintech product's design language and an art-directed editorial site: warm, tactile, precise.

Brand & Visual Direction


Palette: Warm paper/off-white background (not pure white), deep charcoal/ink for primary text, sage-green as the primary accent, marigold/amber as a secondary accent used sparingly for CTAs and highlights. Avoid anything that reads as generic "tech blue."
Typography: Serif display font (Fraunces or similar high-contrast, characterful serif) for headlines and section titles; clean grotesque sans (Inter or similar) for body copy and UI labels; monospace (IBM Plex Mono) for metadata, tags, numbers, and small labels (e.g. "01 —", "Fintech", "3 Months") to give it a systems/product feel.
Grid: Generous whitespace, consistent 8px/4px spacing rhythm, strong left-aligned type hierarchy. Editorial magazine-style layout rather than centered "hero + boxes."
Micro-interactions: Subtle hover states (underline draws in, slight scale on cards, arrow icons that shift on hover), smooth scroll-triggered fade/slide-ins as sections enter viewport, cursor-aware or magnetic hover on primary CTAs. Nothing gimmicky — restrained, premium motion only.
Imagery: Hero portrait/photo treated with a subtle warm duotone or soft-edge mask to match the palette, not a stock-photo drop-shadow card.

Interactivity Requirements


Smooth scroll navigation from nav links to sections
Scroll-triggered entrance animations (fade + slight upward slide) for hero, each case study card, and the "What I Bring" section — staggered timing
Hover states on all interactive elements: buttons scale/shift subtly, links underline-draw, case study cards lift or reveal accent color on hover
Nav bar transitions from transparent to solid/blurred background on scroll
Responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile with the two-column hero stacking cleanly on smaller screens


Overall Feel

Premium, editorial, warm, and precise — like a well-designed fintech product met a design studio's own site. Confident typography, restrained color use, purposeful motion. Should feel like it was designed by someone who deeply understands both product UX and brand craft.
