Writing Style
Write from the perspective of a Senior Product Designer.
Explain why each design decision was made, not just what changed.
Focus on UX principles, information architecture, accessibility, interaction design, and business outcomes.
Avoid marketing language.
Keep the writing concise, insightful, and portfolio-ready.
Make the case study suitable for hiring managers reviewing senior product design work.
Clearly label all metrics as estimated outcomes based on UX best practices unless they are supported by actual analytics or usability testing.
