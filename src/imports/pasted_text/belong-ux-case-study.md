# Prompt: Generate a Senior UX Case Study for Belong Investment App

Create a polished, recruiter-ready UX case study for **Belong**, a mobile fintech application that helps young Africans start investing confidently by making investing feel like a lifestyle choice instead of a financial chore.

The tone should be that of a senior Product Designer explaining the thinking behind the work—not marketing copy. Every section should explain **why decisions were made**, the UX principles behind them, and the expected business impact.

The case study should feel similar to portfolios from designers at Stripe, Google, Airbnb, or Wise: clean, editorial, visual, and focused on design decisions rather than feature descriptions.

---

# Project Overview

Write an engaging overview introducing Belong.

Include:

* Belong is a fintech mobile application.
* It helps young people begin investing and building wealth.
* The goal is to simplify investing by making investment products feel approachable and familiar rather than intimidating.
* Instead of presenting investments as complex financial products, Belong organizes them like music playlists ("Jams"), making investing feel like a lifestyle choice.
* Explain that the design balances regulatory requirements, trust, business goals, and usability.

Also mention that as Product Designer I designed:

* Onboarding
* Identity Verification (KYC)
* Home Dashboard
* Product Detail Pages
* Portfolio Experience

# Writing Style

* Write in first person where appropriate.
* Use professional UX case study language.
* Explain **why** every design decision was made.
* Avoid marketing language.
* Prioritize design rationale over feature descriptions.
* Make the case study feel like it was written by a Senior Product Designer.
* Clearly label all metrics as **estimated outcomes based on UX best practices** unless supported by real usability testing or analytics. This keeps the case study credible while still demonstrating the expected impact of the design decisions.
